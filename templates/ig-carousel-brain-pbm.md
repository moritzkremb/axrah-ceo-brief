## Design Notes

- **Palette:** Black/charcoal base. AXRAH red as primary accent. Warm amber for light/energy visuals. White body text only.
- **Font:** Bold condensed sans-serif for headlines (Bebas Neue, Neue Haas Grotesk Display, or similar). Regular weight for body — never italic except footnotes.
- **Ratio:** 4:5 (1080x1350). Takes more feed real estate, forces tighter copy discipline.
- **Text placement:** Headlines always top-anchored or centre. Never bottom — gets cut on mobile preview.
- **Slide 3 diagram** is the save-driver. Commission it properly — don't use stock. This is the asset that gets shared and screenshotted.
- **Slide 5** is the engagement spike — "what people notice" language breaks the science pattern and pulls in non-biohacker followers. Don't cut it.
- **Disclaimer placement:** Footnote on Slides 3 and 4. Never in the headline tier. Keeps it honest without killing the flow.
