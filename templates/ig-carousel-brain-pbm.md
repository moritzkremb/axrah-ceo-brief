## Design Notes

- **Colour palette:** Deep black / charcoal background, AXRAH brand red, white text. One accent of electric blue on the brain/neuron slides to suggest energy.
- **Font:** Bold sans-serif headlines (e.g. Neue Haas Grotesk or similar). Clean body text, not too small.
- **Slide ratio:** 1:1 (1080x1080) or 4:5 (1080x1350) for feed. 4:5 takes more real estate.
- **Slide 3 diagram** is the key creative asset — worth doing properly. A clean NIR penetration cross-section drives saves and shares.
- **Disclaimer on Slide 4** is non-negotiable — keeps AXRAH on the right side of health marketing rules.
