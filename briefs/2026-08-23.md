- **University of Toronto researchers published in *Brain Stimulation*** showing pulsed PBM (via the Vielight device) measurably boosts cerebrospinal fluid flow — an emerging brain-health/neuro angle for PBM beyond pain, skin, and recovery.
- **Dry AMD trial nuance:** the LightSite III trial data shows multiwavelength PBM improves visual acuity in dry AMD patients but did **not** change drusen (the retinal deposits underlying the disease) — worth knowing so sales/marketing don't overstate the FDA-adjacent AMD story from the last brief; the benefit is functional (vision), not structural.
- **Consumer adoption keeps climbing:** Bon Charge's 2026 Wellness Tech Trend Report finds more than half of Americans under 35 have tried or explored RLT-type wellness tech — the demographic tailwind for the category keeps building.
- **Home-use/portable/patch-based PBM devices are the fastest-growing consumer sub-segment** — reinforces that the low end is commoditizing fast, while AXRAH's clinical/B2B positioning stays differentiated at the high end.
