- **Category growth continues to accelerate.** Multiple market trackers now put the red light therapy beds segment at ~$9.29B in 2026, growing to ~$19.56B by 2033 (13%+ CAGR) — consistent with the thesis in our own materials. Broader PBM device market estimated at $281M (2026) → $515M–$2.4B by 2033/2035 depending on source. [Coherent Market Insights](https://www.coherentmarketinsights.com/industry-reports/red-light-therapy-beds-market), [Vantage Market Research](https://www.vantagemarketresearch.com/photobiomodulation-market)
- **Regulatory line is hardening, and it favors clinical-grade players.** Coverage this year (MedCity News, others) is drawing a sharper public distinction between FDA-cleared (510(k)) devices and General Wellness-exempt consumer gadgets — "FDA Approved" is being called out publicly as incorrect terminology for this category. As scrutiny increases, AXRAH's clinical positioning becomes a differentiator against consumer panel brands that blur this line. [MedCity News](https://medcitynews.com/2026/03/red-light-therapys-regulatory-implications/)

Worth watching as a signal that clinical-grade PBM assets are becoming acquisition targets, not just standalone device sales.
- **DoD is funding PBM research:** $4.6M grant (Aug 2025) to University of Utah/NYU studying Vielight's PBM tech for traumatic brain injury — reinforces the "PBM is real medicine, not wellness fluff" narrative useful in enterprise/military-adjacent B2B conversations.
