## Industry & Market
- RLT adoption keeps climbing: over half of Americans under 35 say they've tried or explored red light therapy or blue-light-blocking tech. Top reported benefits: skin appearance (36%), better sleep (29%), muscle/joint comfort (29%) — good hierarchy for messaging priority.
- PBM market sizing varies by source but agrees on strong double-digit growth: ~$281M (2026) growing at 10.6% CAGR, or ~$2.4B by 2035 at 10.2% CAGR depending on methodology. Directionally this confirms the underlying market thesis.
- Home-use and portable PBM devices are called out as the democratizing trend in the category — accessible price points and formats expanding the buyer base beyond clinics. This favors AXRAH's Panel/Panel Pro price positioning.
- New clinical research: a University of Toronto/Baycrest study used fMRI to show pulsed PBM measurably boosts cerebrospinal fluid flow — an emerging brain-health angle for the category. Separately, the LIGHTSITE III trial reported modest visual acuity improvement in dry AMD patients from PBM, adding another evidence-backed indication.
- FDA reminder: most RLT devices are FDA-*cleared* (510(k), narrow indications like minor pain/stiffness) or merely FDA-*registered* (administrative, not a safety/efficacy review) — not FDA-*approved*. No at-home devices carry full FDA approval. Worth double-checking AXRAH's own claims stay precise on "Class II certified and registered" language versus implying clearance for anti-aging or performance claims, since regulators and competitors alike are visibly scrutinizing this distinction in the press right now.
