## Industry & Market

- Photobiomodulation market sizing continues to firm up: one estimate puts it at ~$281M (2026) growing to ~$515M by 2033 (10.6% CAGR); a separate estimate has PBM at ~$0.9B (2025) → ~$2.4B by 2035 (10.2% CAGR). RLT beds specifically remain on the $8.21B (2025) → $19.30B (2032) track. ([Coherent Market Insights](https://www.coherentmarketinsights.com/industry-reports/photobiomodulation-market), [Vantage Market Research](https://www.vantagemarketresearch.com/photobiomodulation-market))
- Multiwavelength PBM significantly improved best-corrected visual acuity in a dry AMD trial (LightSite III) — another clinical proof point for the category beyond pain/recovery. ([Ophthalmology Advisor](https://www.ophthalmologyadvisor.com/news/photobiomodulation-dry-amd-lightsite-iii-results/))
- University of Toronto (Baycrest/Rotman) researchers showed real-time brain fluid (CSF) dynamics changes from brief pulsed PBM sessions — early but notable neuro/longevity research angle. ([GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/01/08/3215395/0/en/University-of-Toronto-Researchers-Report-Real-Time-Brain-Fluid-Modulation-Using-Vielight-Photobiomodulation.html))
- Equipment trend: the market is moving toward larger panels and connectable multi-panel setups for full-body coverage — validates AXRAH's Grid/Pod line direction. Small (n=20) but encouraging study on PBM for mild cognitive impairment also surfaced. ([Light Therapy Insiders](https://www.lighttherapyinsiders.com/2026-red-light-therapy-panels/))
- Category-level trend: strategic shift toward home-use, portable, and patch-based PBM devices to widen market access beyond clinics — relevant context if/when Halo or SkinIQ move toward launch, but not something to discuss externally yet.
