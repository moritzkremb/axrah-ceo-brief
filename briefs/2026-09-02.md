## Industry & Market

- **PBM market sizing holds its growth story:** global photobiomodulation market est. $281.3M in 2026 → $514.8M by 2033 (10.6% CAGR); RLT beds specifically remain on the $8.21B (2025) → $19.30B by 2032 track (13% CAGR). No change to the macro thesis — the category is still scaling fast.
- **Clinical credibility keeps building:** University of Toronto researchers reported (via fMRI) that pulsed PBM measurably boosts cerebrospinal fluid flow in real time — a brain-health angle beyond the usual pain/recovery framing. Separately, PBM continues to gain traction in ophthalmology (retinopathy of prematurity, diabetic retinopathy, AMD) and remains the standard of care for preventing oral mucositis in cancer patients. ~140 PBM papers were published between July 1 and Aug 20, 2026 alone — this is a maturing clinical field, not a wellness fad.
- **Consumer adoption accelerating, especially under-35s:** Bon Charge's 2026 Wellness Tech Trend Report finds more than half of Americans under 35 have tried or explored red light therapy. Drivers: demand for non-drug recovery/longevity solutions and better tech (higher output, improved wavelength coverage) — both play directly to AXRAH's clinical-grade/B2B positioning.
- **Consumer panel segment is commoditizing fast:** a wave of new entrants (Hooga, Block Blue Light, JOVS) and constant "best panel of 2026" comparison content signals the sub-$1K panel tier is becoming a price war. This reinforces that AXRAH's real differentiation and margin sit in the B2B/clinical tier (Grid, Pod, Pod Ultra), not the entry Panel.
