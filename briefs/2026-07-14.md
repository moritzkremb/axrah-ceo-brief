Broad direction: home-use, portable, and app-connected devices are the fastest-growing segment — the category is being pulled toward accessibility/affordability, which plays to AXRAH's pricing position. ([Coherent Market Insights](https://www.coherentmarketinsights.com/industry-reports/photobiomodulation-market))
