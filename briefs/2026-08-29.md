## Industry & Market

- **Consumer adoption keeps climbing:** new survey data shows more than half of Americans under 35 have tried or explored RLT and blue-light-blocking tools. Category is mainstreaming fast among a demographic AXRAH doesn't primarily sell to (B2B), but it's the pipeline for future gym/med-spa member demand.
- **Market sizing reconfirmed:** RLT beds $8.21B (2025) → $19.30B by 2032 (13% CAGR); separately, photobiomodulation overall is pegged at ~$281M (2026) growing to $514M–$2.4B by 2033–2035 depending on the source, at 10–10.6% CAGR. Directionally consistent — strong double-digit growth either way.
- **New clinical research on cognitive health:** a small (10-per-group) study on photobiomodulation for mild cognitive impairment showed improved memory/cognition markers; separately, University of Toronto researchers published in *Brain Stimulation* showing pulsed PBM measurably boosts cerebrospinal fluid flow (fMRI-confirmed). Both are early-stage but add to the "PBM is crossing into real neuroscience" narrative — useful for clinical credibility content, not yet strong enough to claim outright.
- **Dosing science getting more rigorous:** industry commentary is increasingly flagging that dose-response isn't linear — beyond a threshold, light exposure may be counterproductive. Regulatory and research pressure is pushing toward standardized dosing protocols and higher manufacturing quality bars. This favors AXRAH's clinical-grade positioning over cheap consumer panels with no dosing guidance.
