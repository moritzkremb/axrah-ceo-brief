## Industry & Market

- **Consumer adoption is accelerating.** Bon Charge's 2026 Wellness Tech Trend Report puts more than half of Americans under 35 as having tried or explored red light therapy, with top reported benefits being skin appearance (36%), sleep (29%), and muscle/joint comfort (29%). This is the demographic AXRAH's B2B buyers (gyms, med spas) are chasing — good ammunition for sales conversations about ROI on demand.
- **Market sizing holds up:** photobiomodulation market est. $281.3M (2026) → $514.8M by 2033 (10.6% CAGR) per one estimate, or up to $2.4B by 2035 (10.2% CAGR) per another. RLT beds specifically: $8.21B (2025) → $19.30B by 2032 (13% CAGR) — consistent with prior tracking. ([towardshealthcare.com](https://www.towardshealthcare.com/insights/photomedicine-market-sizing), [vantagemarketresearch.com](https://www.vantagemarketresearch.com/photobiomodulation-market))
- **Research volume is heavy:** ~140 PBM papers published on PubMed between July 1–Aug 20, 2026 alone. Two notable ones: a Vielight study linking PBM to memory/cognitive improvement in mild cognitive impairment, and University of Toronto research (published in *Brain Stimulation*) showing pulsed PBM measurably boosts cerebrospinal fluid flow via fMRI. Neuro/cognitive applications are becoming a credible sub-category — worth watching for a future product angle, not urgent. ([globenewswire.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/01/08/3215395/0/en/University-of-Toronto-Researchers-Report-Real-Time-Brain-Fluid-Modulation-Using-Vielight-Photobiomodulation.html))
- **Regulatory landscape unchanged but worth restating for EU sales:** FDA 510(k) clearance (US) and CE marking under EU MDR (Germany/Austria) remain separate frameworks — a CE-certified device can be sold as a wellness product in the EU but can't carry US "clinically proven" claims without its own FDA 510(k). Make sure sales collateral for German/Austrian prospects doesn't inadvertently borrow US clinical language. ([youlumistore.com](https://www.youlumistore.com/blogs/red-light-therapy-guide/fda-510k-vs-ce-red-light-therapy-certifications))
