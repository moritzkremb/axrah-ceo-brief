## Industry & Market

- **PBM clinical legitimacy keeps compounding.** A University of Toronto study (published in *Brain Stimulation*) used fMRI to show pulsed photobiomodulation measurably boosts cerebrospinal fluid flow in real time — a new, brain-health angle beyond pain/recovery. Separately, the LightSite III trial (results out March 2026) showed multiwavelength PBM significantly improves visual acuity in dry AMD patients, building on the FDA authorization already in AXRAH's talk track.
- **Market sizing still points up but estimates vary by segment** — PBM device market estimated ~$281M (2026) growing to ~$515M by 2033 (10.6% CAGR) in one estimate; broader RLT beds market still tracking toward the $8.21B→$19.3B (13% CAGR) figures already in our materials. Use whichever framing fits the audience; don't mix them in one pitch.
- **Home-use/portable/patch-based PBM is the fastest-growing sub-segment** — manufacturers are chasing affordability and convenience at the low end. This reinforces that the real competitive fight for AXRAH is at the clinical/B2B tier, not the consumer panel tier.
- **Independent spectroscopic testing of commercial devices is becoming a story.** A military research presentation found some products' actual wavelength/power output significantly diverged from their marketing specs (one device beat spec by ~60%, another underperformed by ~two-thirds). Buyers and clinics are increasingly primed to ask "can you prove your specs independently?" — this is a credibility opening.
