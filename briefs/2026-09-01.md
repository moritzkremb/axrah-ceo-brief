## Industry & Market

- **Home/portable PBM is the growth vector.** Multiple market reports point to a strategic shift toward home-use, portable/handheld, and patch-based PBM devices as the segment "democratizing" the category. AXRAH's B2B clinical positioning is still the right hedge against this commoditizing wave at the low end. ([Coherent Market Insights](https://www.coherentmarketinsights.com/industry-reports/photobiomodulation-market))
- **Market sizing reconfirmed, still growing double digits.** Photobiomodulation market ~$281.3M (2026) → $514.8M by 2033 (10.6% CAGR); broader photomedicine market ~$6.06B (2026) → $10.90B by 2035. RLT beds specifically remain on the $8.21B (2025) → $19.30B (2032) track. ([Towards Healthcare](https://www.towardshealthcare.com/insights/photomedicine-market-sizing))
- **Research volume is heavy.** ~140 PBM papers hit PubMed between July 1 and Aug 20, 2026 alone. Notably, University of Toronto researchers published in *Brain Stimulation* showing pulsed PBM measurably boosts cerebrospinal fluid flow via fMRI — a legitimate new mechanism story (brain/cognitive angle, not just recovery/pain). ([GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/01/08/3215395/0/en/University-of-Toronto-Researchers-Report-Real-Time-Brain-Fluid-Modulation-Using-Vielight-Photobiomodulation.html))
- **FDA clearance vs. "approval" confusion is a live marketing/compliance issue industry-wide.** No at-home RLT device is FDA "approved" in the pharmaceutical sense — most are 510(k) cleared or simply Class II registered with no clinical review. Full 510(k) submissions now run ~$50K. Worth a compliance gut-check on how AXRAH's own "FDA Class II Certified" language is phrased on the site and in sales collateral, since sloppy claims here are an easy competitor attack line. ([Wareable](https://www.wareable.com/health-tech/best-fda-cleared-red-light-therapy-devices))
