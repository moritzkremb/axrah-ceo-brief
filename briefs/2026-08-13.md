## Industry & Market

- RLT adoption keeps climbing: over half of Americans under 35 say they've tried or explored red light therapy/light-based recovery tools. Most-cited consumer benefits: skin appearance (36%), sleep (29%), muscle/joint comfort (29%). ([UPR](https://www.upr.org/health/2026-08-11/red-light-therapy-science), [AOL](https://www.aol.com/articles/more-americans-trying-red-light-235953000.html))
- Photobiomodulation market sizing continues to point up — estimates range from ~$281M (2026) growing to ~$515M by 2033 (10.6% CAGR) to a broader $0.9B → $2.4B by 2035 view depending on scope. Consistent driver: portable/handheld and patch-based PBM devices pushing the category out of clinics and into home/outpatient use — same direction AXRAH's panel/mat lineup is aimed at. ([Coherent Market Insights](https://www.coherentmarketinsights.com/industry-reports/photobiomodulation-market), [Vantage Market Research](https://www.vantagemarketresearch.com/photobiomodulation-market))
- Notable clinical news: University of Toronto researchers published fMRI evidence that brief pulsed PBM sessions measurably boost cerebrospinal fluid flow — a new angle (brain/CSF) beyond the usual recovery/skin claims. Separate trial (LIGHTSITE III) showed visual acuity benefit from multiwavelength PBM in dry AMD patients. Useful if AXRAH wants a "science is expanding beyond recovery" talking point. ([GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/01/08/3215395/0/en/University-of-Toronto-Researchers-Report-Real-Time-Brain-Fluid-Modulation-Using-Vielight-Photobiomodulation.html), [Ophthalmology Advisor](https://www.ophthalmologyadvisor.com/news/photobiomodulation-dry-amd-lightsite-iii-results/))
