Top reported benefits: skin appearance (36%), sleep (29%), muscle/joint comfort (29%). ([AOL](https://www.aol.com/articles/more-americans-trying-red-light-235953000.html))
- Photobiomodulation market estimated at **$281M in 2026, growing to $514.8M by 2033** (10.6% CAGR); a separate longer-horizon forecast puts the broader photomedicine market at $2.4B+ by 2035. Market driver: chronic disease management + non-invasive procedure adoption. ([Coherent Market Insights](https://www.coherentmarketinsights.com/industry-reports/photobiomodulation-market))

A separate trial (LIGHTSITE III) showed modest visual acuity benefit for dry AMD patients without disease progression change. ([GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/01/08/3215395/0/en/University-of-Toronto-Researchers-Report-Real-Time-Brain-Fluid-Modulation-Using-Vielight-Photobiomodulation.html), [Ophthalmology Advisor](https://www.ophthalmologyadvisor.com/news/photobiomodulation-dry-amd-lightsite-iii-results/))
