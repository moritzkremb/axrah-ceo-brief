## Competitor Intelligence
- **NovoTHOR — FDA Class 2 recall, second one this year.** The FDA posted a new Class 2 device recall for the NovoTHOR Whole Body Light Pod Gen 3.0 on Aug 6, 2026 (a prior NovoTHOR recall was updated in May). A related Canadian recall on an earlier generation cited failed ball studs causing the canopy's gas strut to detach — a mechanical/hardware reliability issue, not a light-output problem. Still, two recalls in one year on a $65K+ device is a credibility hit worth using directly against them with prospects comparing pod options. ([FDA record](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfres/res.cfm?id=205315))

Note: this is independent third-party test data, not an AXRAH claim, so it's safe to reference and cite the source directly.
