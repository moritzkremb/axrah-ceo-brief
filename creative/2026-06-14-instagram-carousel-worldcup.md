## NOTES FOR EDITOR

- Slide 3 and Slide 4 are the core IP slides — do not cut these if the deck is shortened
- Slide 4 competitor pricing must carry the "market estimates" footnote for legal accuracy
- The World Cup visual language (stadium, boots, pitch) should run through every slide even where product takes centre stage — do not let it drop after slide 2
- All slides should feel like they belong to the same campaign: dark, high-contrast, minimal text, confident
- No stock photo energy — this should feel editorial/campaign, not marketing brochure
